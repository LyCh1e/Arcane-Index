# Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)

## What It Is

The **Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)** is an open governance body under the **Linux Foundation**, established in December 2025 to steward the Model Context Protocol and related agentic AI standards.

## Formation

Announced 9 December 2025 when Anthropic donated the Model Context Protocol to the organisation. The formation was simultaneously announced by the Linux Foundation.

**Co-founders**: Anthropic, OpenAI, Block.

**Supporting members**: AWS, Google DeepMind, Microsoft, Cloudflare, GitHub, Bloomberg.

## Projects Under AAIF

- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — the primary project
- **goose** — an open-source AI agent
- **AGENTS.md** — a standard for agent capability declarations

## Governance Model

The existing MCP maintainer group (9 core leaders, 58 total maintainers as of Nov 2025) continues to operate. Governance priorities: community input, transparent decision-making, and enterprise readiness.

## Significance

Transitioning MCP from an Anthropic-owned protocol to a neutral, multi-stakeholder open standard reduces vendor lock-in concerns and enables broader enterprise adoption. The involvement of both Anthropic and OpenAI as co-founders is notable given that they are otherwise primary competitors.
