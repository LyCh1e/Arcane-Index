# Accenture PTE LTD

## Identity

Accenture PTE LTD is the Singapore-registered private limited company through which Accenture plc — the global professional services firm — operates in Singapore and leads its South-east Asia commercial activities.

## Parent: Accenture plc (Global)

Accenture PTE LTD is a subsidiary of Accenture plc, a publicly listed company (NYSE: ACN) incorporated in Ireland. The parent is a member of the S&P 100, Russell 1000, and Fortune Global 500.

## History

Accenture traces its origins to the business and technology consulting division of accounting firm Arthur Andersen, established formally as Andersen Consulting from 1989 to 2000. The Singapore entity was incorporated in October 1975, making it one of Accenture's earliest international presences.

A dispute over profit-sharing arrangements between Andersen Consulting and Arthur Andersen escalated into international arbitration. In August 2000, the International Chamber of Commerce ruled that Andersen Consulting must pay Arthur Andersen US$1.2 billion in back payments and must relinquish the Andersen name. On 1 January 2001, the firm rebranded globally as **Accenture**. The timing proved fortuitous: Arthur Andersen collapsed in 2002 following federal criminal charges linked to the Enron scandal.

## Singapore Operations

Accenture has operated in Singapore since 1975 — one of its longest-established international presences. The Singapore office serves as the headquarters for South-east Asia operations.

## Notable Singapore Projects

**UOB Digital Transformation Partnership (April 2025)**
Three-year MoU — the first between Accenture and a Singapore bank. UOB uses Accenture's AI Refinery™ platform to accelerate GenAI adoption across business processes, risk management, customer personalisation, and employee upskilling via LearnVantage.

**HTX Public Safety Initiative (2025)**
MoU with Singapore's Home Team Science and Technology Agency to fast-track AI and data solutions for public safety. Involves a global network spanning government agencies, academia, and start-ups in Singapore and the United Kingdom.

**Changi Airport Group — DIVA Digital Factory (ongoing)**
Long-running engagement. Accenture built and operates DIVA, an AI-powered digital factory that drives continuous innovation in passenger experience and airport operations.

## Competitive Position (Global)

Accenture competes with IBM Consulting, Deloitte, McKinsey & Company, PwC, BCG, and TCS. With FY2025 revenue of US$69.67 billion, Accenture leads all competitors by total revenue. In AI consulting specifically, it holds approximately 22% market share. In pure strategy consulting, McKinsey ($18.8 billion), BCG ($14.1 billion), and Accenture Strategy & Consulting ($14 billion) are closely matched.

## Organisational Structure (effective 1 September 2025)

Accenture consolidated its five former service lines (Strategy, Consulting, Song, Technology, Operations) into a single integrated unit: **Reinvention Services**, led by Manish Sharma as Chief Services Officer. The rationale is faster, AI-embedded solution delivery.

Reinvention Services comprises seven capability areas: Cybersecurity, Digital Core, Finance, Industry & Enterprise, Song, Supply Chain & Engineering, and Talent.

The business continues to be managed and reported through three geographic markets: **Americas**, **EMEA**, and **Asia Pacific**.

## Acquisitions Strategy

Accenture operates an active M&A programme, acquiring firms to expand capability rather than geographic footprint. Recent acquisitions relevant to AI and creative:

- **Faculty** (UK, announced January 2026) — AI native services firm specialising in safe and ethical AI, decision intelligence. 400+ AI professionals. Founder Marc Warner became CTO.
- **Whalar** (announced 2026) — creator and social agency, expanding Accenture Song.
- **Superdigital** (2025) — digital marketing capability for Song.
- **Unlimited** (2024) — marketing and creative.
