# Vitalik Buterin

## Who

Vitalik Buterin (born 1994, Canada/Russia) is the co-founder and primary architect of Ethereum. He proposed Ethereum in late 2013 and has continued as its de facto technical leader and researcher.

## Key Contributions

| Contribution | Date | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Ethereum Whitepaper | December 2013 | Proposed a "world computer" — Turing-complete blockchain with smart contracts |
| Ethereum mainnet launch | July 2015 | First production smart contract platform |
| Blockchain Trilemma | ~2018 | Coined the term: decentralisation × security × scalability cannot all be maximised simultaneously |
| EIP-1559 (co-authored) | August 2021 | Reformed ETH fee market — base fee burned, reduced issuance |
| The Merge (advocacy) | September 2022 | Led Ethereum's transition from PoW to PoS, reducing energy use ~99.9% |
| Proto-Danksharding / EIP-4844 | March 2024 | Introduced "blobs" — cheap data availability for rollups, dramatically cutting L2 fees |

## Key Ideas

- **Blockchain Trilemma**: The core insight that blockchains cannot simultaneously maximise decentralisation, security, and scalability — forcing trade-offs in all designs.
- **Endgame**: Vitalik's 2021 essay arguing that all roads in blockchain scaling lead to a rollup-centric future secured by a small set of block producers but verified by ZK proofs — preserving trustlessness without sacrificing throughput.
- **Account Abstraction (ERC-4337)**: Vision for programmable wallet logic — enabling social recovery, gas sponsorship, and batch transactions without protocol changes.
- **The Merge**: Ethereum's consensus change from PoW to PoS represented the largest engineering migration in blockchain history — a live upgrade on a $200B+ network.

## Role in Ethereum Governance

Ethereum has no formal CEO or controller. Buterin operates as a highly influential researcher and writer. Core protocol changes require rough consensus across client teams, researchers, and the community via **EIPs** (Ethereum Improvement Proposals). His public endorsements carry significant but not absolute weight.
