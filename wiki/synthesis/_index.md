## How Synthesis Pages Work

A synthesis page answers: *"Given everything I know about X, what is the current picture?"*

It is:
- Rewritten, not appended — it reflects the current best understanding
- Linked to the concepts and sources it draws from
- Flagged with `[!contradiction]` callouts where sources disagree
- Marked with `[!gap]` callouts where knowledge is missing
