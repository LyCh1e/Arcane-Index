# Research: Accenture PTE LTD

## Overview

Accenture PTE LTD (UEN: 197501877H) is the Singapore-registered subsidiary of Accenture plc, a global professional services firm. Incorporated in 1975, it is one of Accenture's oldest international entities and serves as the South-east Asia hub. The parent company generates approximately US$69.67 billion annually (FY2025), employs ~799,000 people globally, and is restructuring around a unified "Reinvention Services" model centred on AI.

---

## Key Findings

### Corporate Identity

- Accenture PTE LTD (Singapore) was incorporated **28 October 1975** as Arthur Andersen Associates PTE LTD, later renamed Andersen Consulting Singapore PTE LTD, then Accenture PTE LTD after the global rebrand on 1 January 2001. (Source: SGP Business, OpenCorporates; confidence: **high**)
- Registered address: 250 North Bridge Road, #35-00, Raffles City Tower, Singapore 179101. (confidence: **high**)
- The controlling entity is **Accenture Public Limited Company** (Ireland). NYSE ticker: ACN.
- The firm globally belongs to the S&P 100, Russell 1000, and Fortune Global 500.

### History and Rebrand

- Andersen Consulting separated from Arthur Andersen following a bitter arbitration, ruled by the International Chamber of Commerce in August 2000. Andersen Consulting paid US$1.2 billion and forfeited the Andersen name.
- The January 2001 rebrand to "Accenture" distanced the firm from Arthur Andersen, which subsequently collapsed in 2002 due to its role in the Enron accounting scandal. (Source: Britannica, Wikipedia; confidence: **high**)
- The name "Accenture" was coined internally — a portmanteau of "Accent on the future". (Source: Yahoo Finance 25th-anniversary article; confidence: **medium**)

### Global Financial Profile

- FY2025 annual revenue: **US$69.67 billion** (+7.36% YoY). (Source: SEC 10-K, Macrotrends; confidence: **high**)
- Q3 FY2026 quarterly revenue: **US$18.7 billion** (+3% local currency, +6% USD YoY). (Source: Accenture Fact Sheet; confidence: **high**)
- FY2025 global headcount: ~779,000; Q3 FY2026: ~799,000. (confidence: **high**)
- Clients served: ~9,000 globally. (confidence: **high**)
- Revenue by geography (Q3 FY2026): Americas 49%, EMEA 37%, Asia Pacific 14%. (confidence: **high**)
- Revenue by industry (Q3 FY2026): Products 30%, Health & Public Service 21%, Financial Services 19%, Comms/Media/Tech 17%, Resources 13%. (confidence: **high**)

### Singapore and South-east Asia Operations

- Accenture has operated in Singapore since 1975 — 51 years of continuous presence as of 2026.
- SEA scope: **331 clients** across **19 industries**, **17,000+ employees**, **75 nationalities**. (Source: Accenture Singapore page; confidence: **medium** — figures likely updated periodically)
- Two Singapore offices at Raffles City Tower, one functioning as an innovation hub.
- Additional SEA offices: Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Bangkok.

### Organisational Restructure (September 2025)

Accenture merged its five service lines (Strategy, Consulting, Song, Technology, Operations) into a single integrated unit called **Reinvention Services** (effective 1 September 2025). This unit is led by Manish Sharma as the first Chief Services Officer. The seven capability areas within Reinvention Services are: Cybersecurity, Digital Core, Finance, Industry & Enterprise, Song, Supply Chain & Engineering, and Talent. (Source: Consultancy.com.au, The Consulting Report; confidence: **high**)

Reporting continues via three geographic markets: Americas, EMEA, Asia Pacific.

### AI Strategy

- Accenture holds approximately **22% AI consulting market share** as of 2025. (Source: 1840&Co; confidence: **medium** — derived figure, not independently audited)
- Proprietary platform: **AI Refinery™** — used by clients including UOB to deploy GenAI at scale.
- Acquisition of **Faculty** (UK, January 2026): 400+ AI professionals, specialising in safe and ethical AI and the Faculty Frontier decision intelligence platform. Faculty's founder Marc Warner became Accenture's CTO.
- Recognised as a Leader in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Public Cloud IT Transformation Services, placing highest on the Ability to Execute axis.

### Acquisitions Strategy

Accenture pursues aggressive capability-led M&A, typically completing dozens of acquisitions annually. Recent examples: Faculty (AI safety, 2026), Whalar (creator economy, 2026), Superdigital (digital marketing, 2025), Unlimited (creative, 2024). Deal values are rarely disclosed.
