URL: https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Security_Terminology_Cheat_Sheet.html

## Summary

A concise cheat sheet defining security terms that are frequently confused, even by experienced developers. Covers four groups: data handling transforms, cryptography primitives, identity concepts, and federated identity vocabulary.

**Purpose stated by OWASP:** Understanding these terms is critical for correctly implementing security controls and following standards like the OWASP ASVS.
