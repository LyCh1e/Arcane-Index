# ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) v4.0

The ISTQB CTFL v4.0 is the 2024 revision of the internationally recognised entry-level software testing certification. It integrates Agile testing practices with timeless testing fundamentals and is the basis for the PSM/ISTQB Foundation examination worldwide.

**English exams available**: May 2024. **Minimum training time**: 1,135 minutes (~19 hours).

---

## Syllabus Structure (Six Chapters)

1. Fundamentals of Testing
2. Testing Throughout the Software Development Lifecycle
3. Static Testing
4. Test Analysis and Design
5. Managing the Test Activities
6. Test Tools
