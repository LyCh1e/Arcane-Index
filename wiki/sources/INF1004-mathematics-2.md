# INF1004 Mathematics 2

**Institution:** Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT)
**Lecturer:** Ryan Fraser Kirwan

## Module Structure

Two-part module covering applied mathematics for computing:

| Part | Topics | Weeks |
|------|--------|-------|
| Part 1 — Statistics & Probability | Descriptive statistics, probability theory, discrete & continuous distributions, sampling | 1–6 |
| Part 2 — Linear Algebra | Matrices, SLE, vectors, transformations, eigenvalues, SVD | 8–13 |

## Assessment

- **iRATs and tRATs** (Weeks 8–13): 25% (5% each)
- **Exam** (Week 14): 50% (25% per part)
