# Source: Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker (2026)

## Summary

The Atlantic Council's GeoEconomics Center maintains the most widely-cited real-time tracker of central bank digital currency (CBDC) development globally. It tracks country-by-country progress across research, development, pilot, and launch stages.

## Global Status (as of 2026)

| Stage | Count |
|---|---|
| Exploring (any phase) | 146 countries / currency unions |
| Advanced (development, pilot, or launched) | 77 countries |
| Pilot active | 41 projects |
| Fully launched (retail) | 3 (Bahamas, Jamaica, Nigeria) |
| G20 members beyond research phase | 18 of 20 |

GDP coverage: **over 98 % of global GDP** is exploring a CBDC (up from 35 countries in 2020).

## Launched Retail CBDCs

| Country | Name | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Bahamas | Sand Dollar | Live |
| Jamaica | JAM-DEX | Live |
| Nigeria | eNaira | Live (adoption limited) |

Nigeria's eNaira recorded approximately ₦22 billion in transactions through 2025. Adoption has been concentrated in government airdrops rather than organic retail use.

## Major Projects

### China — e-CNY (Digital Yuan)
- 3.4 billion transactions by December 2025
- Cumulative value: **16.7 trillion renminbi** (~US$2.3 trillion)
- Expanding via **Project mBridge** (cross-border, with HK, UAE, Thailand, Saudi Arabia)

### European Union
- ECB concluded two-year preparation phase October 2025
- 2026 issuance decision pending
- Digital euro would be programmable but privacy-preserving by design

### United Kingdom
- Bank of England and HM Treasury in deep design phase
- No build decision as of 2026; progress update expected mid-2026

### United States
- **GENIUS Act** (signed July 18, 2025) explicitly **bans** the Federal Reserve from issuing a retail CBDC
- Focuses US digital currency strategy on stablecoin regulation instead

## Cross-Border Wholesale CBDCs

13 wholesale CBDC projects exist globally. mBridge (China, Hong Kong, UAE, Thailand, Saudi Arabia) is the largest:
- Transaction volume: **$55.49 billion** (a 2,500× increase since 2022 pilot)
- All 11 BRICS members explore CBDCs; 9 are in active pilots
