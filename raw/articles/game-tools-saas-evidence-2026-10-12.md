# Game-community tool evidence (2026-10-12)

## Discord monetisation — dodopayments.com/blogs/monetize-discord-server
- Discord native server subscriptions: Discord keeps 10%; availability limited; little checkout flexibility. Patreon fee 5–12%.
- Paid community price ranges: hobby/interest $3–$9/month; educational $9–$19; professional $19–$49; investment/trading $29–$99; mastermind $99–$299.
- Paid Discord communities see about 8–15% monthly churn; the article advises 100–200 active free members before launching paid tiers.
- The page does not cover premium bot tiers.
