# Market evidence gathered for the simple-SaaS research (2026-10-12)

Facts below were read from the pages named. Most are vendor or blog claims, not audited figures.

## Micro-SaaS patterns — https://redwerk.com/blog/micro-saas-ideas-that-print-money/
- Article's own estimates (not verified): 12 "boring" niches, e.g. compliance tracking for insurance agencies ($9K–25K MRR), licence/certification renewal for trades ($7K–18K), dues and recertification billing ($7K–20K), photo inspection reports ($6K–16K). Dental payroll reconciliation priced at $99–$199 per office.
- Patterns: recurring unavoidable pain; underserved middle market; switching costs; niche regulatory rules; renewal cycles. Validate with 10–20 customers and pre-orders before building. MicroConf: 56% of bootstrapped founders found the idea from a problem they lived.

## Micro-SaaS price points — https://dodopayments.com/blogs/micro-saas-ideas-2026
- Dev tools $19–$39/month (webhook dashboard $29–$79, env-variable manager $19, DB schema docs $39 per connection, git blame report $29 per repo).
- AI wrappers $15–$49/month per seat or per set. Course completion emails $25/month.
- General claims: well-niched products reach roughly $2K–10K MRR in 12–18 months; micro-SaaS sells for 2–4× annual revenue.

## RAG chatbot pricing — https://app.ailog.fr/en/blog/guides/rag-pricing-guide-2026 (vendor-authored by Ailog)
- Monthly tiers: Chatbase $19/$99/$399; CustomGPT $49/$99/$299; Botpress $0/$495; Voiceflow $50/$125/$625; Tidio $29/$59/$398. Intercom Fin $0.99 per resolution; Zendesk AI about $1 per automated resolution.
- Running costs: LLM tokens about $2–5 per 1,000 conversations on budget models vs $30–80 on premium; vector DB about $15–70/month per 1M vectors.
- Article estimate: ~$120,000/year for per-resolution tools at 15,000 conversations/month. Few EU-hosted options.

## Credentialing — https://www.getapp.sg/software/2064207/accredible
- Accredible starts at US$996 (a reviewer paid just under US$1,000/year; LinkedIn sharing needed a higher tier). Integrations include Moodle, Canvas, Thinkific, Teachable. Singapore also has government-backed OpenCerts (SSG newsroom) and local vendors such as Accredify.

## LLM security testing — https://www.modern-datatools.com/tools/promptbrake/pricing
- PromptBrake: free trial; $99 for a 30-day pack (5 scans); $499/month Enterprise (30 scans, 138 checks, CI gates). Priced for deliberate testing, flat not per seat, positioned as complementary to tools such as Snyk. Tests LLM endpoints, not uploaded documents.
