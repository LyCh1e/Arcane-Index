# Non-game niche SaaS evidence (2026-10-12)

## Paid Obsidian plugins — DEV Community pre-sell post (self-reported)
- Pre-sell approach: $19 for the first 20 buyers, planned $39 final price; build only after a second sale; refund if undelivered. Zero sales in four weeks means switching idea.
- Price points: SystemSculpt $19/month or $149 lifetime; MeetingMind Pro $39 lifetime; typical Gumroad range $29–$149.
- Obsidian has 1,800+ community plugins and no official paid marketplace. Self-reported single-developer income $300–$4,500/month; the top plugin's reported $4,200 first month is unsourced. The author's earlier free plugin had 500 downloads and earned $0.

## Temperature monitoring — Operandio landing page (vendor)
- IoT probes and sensors for walk-ins, fridges and freezers; alerts by SMS, email or app that become corrective-action tasks; digital HACCP checklists; LoRaWAN support; works with existing sensor brands.
- Price per site, not published; sold to multi-location restaurant operators. Compliance driver: HACCP, with FSMA referenced.

## Underserved vertical niches — hyscaler.com (blog; no pricing)
- Vertical SaaS cited as growing 18–32% a year versus 12–15% for horizontal tools.
- Listed niches include predictive maintenance for small manufacturers (20–200 employees), livestock health monitoring, equipment maintenance for fleets of 15+ units, SMB cash-flow forecasting ($1M–$20M revenue businesses), vendor risk monitoring, grant management, and trade certification management. Reasons given are that enterprise tools are too costly or heavy for small operators.
