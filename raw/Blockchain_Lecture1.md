Wallets
•Digital Storage: Wallets serve as digital storage for 
cryptocurrencies, allowing users to store, send, and receive 
digital assets. 
•Private and Public Keys: Manages a pair of cryptographic 
keys: a public key, which is like an address that others see 
and send assets to, and a private key, which is known only to 
the owner and grants access to the wallet.

## Slide 55

Connect With Me On Twitter | @HendrikEbbers
Wallets
•Hardware Wallets: Physical devices that store the user's 
private keys offline. Highly secure against online hacking 
since they are cold storage (not connected to the internet). 
•Paper Wallets: Physical documents containing a public 
address for receiving cryptocurrency along with a private 
key for accessing the funds. Also considered a form of cold 
storage and secure against online hacking but vulnerable to 
physical damage or loss.

## Slide 56

Connect With Me On Twitter | @HendrikEbbers
Wallets
•Desktop Wallets: Software wallets installed on a personal 
computer or laptop. Only accessible from the device on 
which they are installed, offering a decent level of security.
•Mobile Wallets: Similar to Desktop Wallets. Smartphone 
apps that store cryptocurrency.
•Web Wallets: Accessed through web browsers. Convenient 
for quick access but potentially vulnerable to online hacking 
and phishing scams.

## Slide 57

Connect With Me On Twitter | @HendrikEbbers
Wallets
•Hot Wallets: Wallets that are connected to the internet, 
including web wallets and some desktop and mobile wallets. 
Vulnerable to online attacks but convenient for frequent 
transactions.
•Cold Wallets: Wallets not connected to the internet, 
including hardware and paper wallets. Provide better 
security against online attacks but less convenient for 
frequent transactions.

## Slide 58

Connect With Me On Twitter | @HendrikEbbers
Wallets
•Security Measures: Incorporates security features such as 
encryption, two-factor authentication, and backup options to 
protect assets and ensure secure access. 
•Multi-Signature Support: Some wallets offer multi-signature 
support, requiring multiple parties to authorize a transaction, 
enhancing security measures. 
•Integration with Exchanges and Platforms: Often integrated with 
cryptocurrency exchanges and platforms to facilitate easy and quick 
transactions and asset management.
